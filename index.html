Featured announcement

KQC Quantum, Inc. and Charlton Aria Acquisition Corporation announce definitive business combination agreement

October 7, 2026

KQC Quantum, Inc., the Delaware parent company of Korea Quantum Computing Co., Ltd., and Charlton Aria Acquisition Corporation (Nasdaq: CHAR) have entered into a definitive business combination agreement. On completion, shares of the combined company are expected to trade on The Nasdaq Stock Market under the ticker symbol “KQC.” The transaction values KQC at a pre-money equity value of approximately $80 million at $11.00 per share, with existing KQC shareholders rolling 100% of their equity into the combined company. Closing is expected in the first half of 2027, subject to Charlton Aria shareholder approval, an extension of Charlton Aria’s business combination deadline and other customary conditions.
